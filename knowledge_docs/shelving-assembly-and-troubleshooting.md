# Shelving Assembly and Troubleshooting

Harbor & Pine flat-pack shelving is sold through Amazon. This guide provides assembly guidance, explains how to identify panel numbers, and covers basic troubleshooting. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Assembly Guidance

1. Clear a clean, flat workspace. Lay out all panels and hardware.
2. Verify all parts against the parts list included in the box. Do not begin assembly if panels or hardware are missing or damaged.
3. Identify each panel by its part number before assembly (see "How to Identify Panel Numbers" below).
4. Follow the printed assembly instructions included with the unit.
5. Use only the hardware provided; do not substitute screws or fasteners.
6. Tighten hardware securely but do not overtighten, which can crack panels.
7. Place the assembled unit on a level surface. Do not exceed the weight capacity listed in the instructions.
8. Do not climb on or stand on the shelving unit.

## How to Identify Panel Numbers

- Panels are marked with a small part number, often printed near an edge or on the back side.
- Common panel labels include: top panel, bottom panel, left side panel, right side panel, back panel, shelf panel, and cube divider panels.
- Part numbers may look like "P1," "P2," "S1," "S2," or similar, depending on the variant.
- Match each panel to the diagram and part list in the assembly instructions before installing.
- If a panel number is illegible or missing, compare the panel's size and shape to the assembly diagram. Do not guess.

## Basic Troubleshooting

| Issue | Possible Cause | Suggested Step |
|---|---|---|
| Panels do not align | Wrong panel installed, or hardware not seated | Re-check panel numbers; re-seat hardware |
| Screws will not tighten | Stripped hole, wrong screw, or overtightened | Stop; do not force. Contact support |
| Unit wobbles | Uneven floor or loose hardware | Level the surface; tighten hardware |
| Cracked or split panel | Impact during shipping or assembly | Stop assembly; follow cracked panel workflow |
| Missing screw or hardware | Hardware omitted at packing | Follow missing hardware workflow |

## Safety Guidance

- Do not use shelving with cracked, split, or otherwise structurally damaged panels.
- Do not exceed the posted weight capacity.
- Do not climb on or stand on the unit.
- Secure tall units to the wall if recommended in the instructions.
- A shelving structural failure or risk of injury requires immediate human escalation.

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may share assembly steps, panel-number identification help, and basic troubleshooting. A human support agent is required for cracked or split panels, missing hardware, structural-failure concerns, any safety incident, and any refund or replacement request.
