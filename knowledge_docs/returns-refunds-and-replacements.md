# Returns, Refunds, and Replacements

Harbor & Pine sells through Amazon. All Harbor & Pine orders are placed through Amazon; Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront. This guide describes return, refund, and replacement boundaries and the evidence required. Harbor & Pine does not promise automatic refunds, replacements, or discounts.

## Return/Refund/Replacement Boundaries

- All remedies are reviewed case by case. Harbor & Pine does not issue automatic refunds, replacements, or discounts.
- Customers should use the Amazon return process within Amazon's stated return window.
- Harbor & Pine support reviews claims outside the Amazon return window on a case-by-case basis.
- A refund or replacement request above $150 requires mandatory human escalation.
- Harbor & Pine cannot force Amazon to issue a refund, replacement, or return label. Any Amazon action is at Amazon's discretion.

## Evidence Required

To review a return, refund, or replacement request, Harbor & Pine support may require:

- Amazon order ID
- Product variant and finish (shelving) or board size (paddle boards)
- Panel number (for shelving panel issues)
- Clear photos of damage, missing parts, or wrong finish
- Packing slip or shipping label photo, if available
- Description of the issue and when it was discovered

Without sufficient evidence, Harbor & Pine cannot review the claim.

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may explain return, refund, and replacement boundaries, list required evidence, and explain that remedies are reviewed case by case. Automation must never promise a refund, replacement, discount, or Amazon action.

A human support agent is required for:

- Any refund or replacement request above $150.
- Chargebacks, payment disputes, or Amazon A-to-z Guarantee claims.
- Legal threats, safety incidents, injuries, or serious complaints.
- Suspected fraud or repeated abuse.
- Personal-data requests.
- Any policy exception.
- Paddle board safety concerns beyond ordinary setup or troubleshooting.
- Repeated defects after one completed troubleshooting attempt.
- Shelving structural failure or risk of injury.

## Mandatory Human Escalation

Escalate to a human support agent immediately for any of the triggers listed above. Automation should acknowledge the customer's request, explain that it requires human review, and set expectations that a support agent will follow up. Automation must not state that a refund or replacement is approved.
