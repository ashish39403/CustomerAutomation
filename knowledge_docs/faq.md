## General Answers Automation May Give

Automation may answer these questions using the listed source files. Automation must never promise a refund, replacement, discount, or Amazon action. If a question touches a mandatory human-escalation trigger (for example, a refund over $150, a safety incident, or a legal threat), automation must escalate to a human support agent instead of answering directly.
