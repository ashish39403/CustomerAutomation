# Paddle Board Setup, Care, and Troubleshooting

Harbor & Pine inflatable paddle boards are sold through Amazon. This guide covers inflation, setup, PSI guidance, leak troubleshooting, cleaning, storage, and warranty-style limitations. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Inflation and Setup Steps

1. Unroll the board on a clean, flat, debris-free surface. Avoid sharp objects, gravel, and hot pavement.
2. Confirm the valve is in the closed (up) position before attaching the pump.
3. Attach the pump hose securely to the valve.
4. Inflate the board using the included pump or an electric pump.
5. Monitor pressure with the gauge. Do not overinflate.
6. Once at target PSI, close the valve cap and attach the fin (if removable) and leash.
7. Check that the board feels firm and holds pressure before entering the water.

## PSI Guidance

| Board Size | Recommended PSI | Maximum PSI |
|---|---|---|
| 10'6" | 12–15 PSI | 15 PSI |
| 11' | 12–15 PSI | 15 PSI |
| 12'6" | 12–15 PSI | 15 PSI |

- Inflate to the recommended PSI range printed on the board or product listing.
- Never exceed the maximum PSI. Overinflation can damage seams and voids warranty-style coverage.
- In hot sun, pressure can rise. Deflate slightly if the board will sit in direct heat.
- In cold water, pressure may drop slightly; top off as needed before paddling.

## Leak Troubleshooting

If the board is losing air:

1. Fully inflate the board to recommended PSI.
2. Listen and feel for escaping air around the valve, seams, and fin box.
3. Apply a mild soapy water solution to suspected areas. Bubbles indicate a leak.
4. Check the valve: ensure it is closed tightly and the valve insert is seated.
5. Mark the leak location with a non-permanent marker.
6. For small punctures, use the included repair kit following the kit instructions.
7. For seam leaks, valve failures, or large tears, do not attempt a field repair. Contact Harbor & Pine support through Amazon.

## Cleaning

- Rinse the board with fresh water after each use, especially after saltwater.
- Use mild soap and a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid harsh solvents, bleach, or abrasive brushes.
- Rinse thoroughly and dry completely before storage.

## Storage

- Store clean, dry, and either partially inflated or loosely rolled.
- Keep out of direct sunlight and extreme heat.
- Store in a cool, dry place away from sharp objects and chemicals.
- Do not store fully inflated in a hot car or garage; heat expands air and can stress seams.

## Warranty-Style Limitations

- Coverage applies to manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- Coverage does not apply to damage from overinflation, contact with sharp objects, misuse, improper storage, or normal wear and tear.
- Harbor & Pine does not promise automatic refunds, replacements, or repairs. Any remedy is reviewed case by case and may require evidence.
- Claims related to safety concerns must be escalated to a human support agent (see Escalation Policy).

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may share inflation steps, PSI guidance, leak-check instructions, cleaning and storage tips, and warranty-style limitations. A human support agent is required for seam leaks, valve failures, suspected manufacturing defects, any safety concern, and any request for a refund or replacement.
