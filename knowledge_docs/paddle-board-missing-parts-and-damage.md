# Paddle Board Missing Parts and Damage

Harbor & Pine inflatable paddle boards are sold through Amazon. This guide covers handling for missing pump, fin, or repair kit, and damage-photo requirements. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Missing Parts Handling

### Missing Pump

- Confirm the pump is not stored inside the board bag or rolled into the board.
- If the pump is genuinely missing, the customer must provide the Amazon order ID and photos showing the unopened box contents.
- Harbor & Pine may arrange a replacement pump subject to review. A human support agent handles the decision.

### Missing Fin

- Confirm the fin is not packed separately in the accessory bag.
- If the fin is missing, the customer must provide the Amazon order ID, board size, and photos of the fin box and accessories.
- A human support agent determines whether a replacement fin can be sent.

### Missing Repair Kit

- Confirm the repair kit is not in a small pouch inside the board bag.
- If missing, the customer must provide the Amazon order ID and photos of the unboxed contents.
- Harbor & Pine may provide repair-kit instructions or a replacement kit, reviewed case by case.

## Damage-Photo Requirements

When reporting damage, the customer must provide clear photos that include:

1. The full board laid flat so the overall condition is visible.
2. A close-up of the damaged area (leak, tear, puncture, seam split, valve damage).
3. A close-up showing any label, size marking, or identifying mark on the board.
4. A photo of the pump, fin, and repair kit if parts are reported missing.
5. A photo showing the Amazon shipping label or packing slip, if available.

Photos must be well-lit, in focus, and show the damage clearly. Blurry or partial photos may delay review. Harbor & Pine does not promise a replacement or refund based on photos alone; all damage claims are reviewed by a human support agent.

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may explain what to check for missing parts, what photos to provide, and how to submit a damage report through Amazon. A human support agent is required to review damage photos, decide on replacement parts, approve any refund or replacement, and handle any safety concern or suspected manufacturing defect.

## Mandatory Human Escalation

Escalate to a human support agent immediately for:
- Paddle board safety concerns beyond ordinary setup or troubleshooting.
- Any suspected manufacturing defect or seam/valve failure.
- Any refund or replacement request above $150.
- Chargebacks, payment disputes, or Amazon A-to-z Guarantee claims.
- Legal threats, safety incidents, injuries, or serious complaints.
- Suspected fraud or repeated abuse.
- Any policy exception.
