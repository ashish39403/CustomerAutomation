# Shelving Missing Parts, Damage, and Wrong Finish

Harbor & Pine flat-pack shelving is sold through Amazon. This guide covers cracked panel, missing screw/hardware, and wrong finish/color workflows, plus damage-photo requirements. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Cracked Panel Workflow

1. Stop assembly immediately. Do not use a unit with a cracked or split panel.
2. Photograph the cracked panel, including the panel number and the full unit in context.
3. Provide the Amazon order ID, variant, finish, and panel number.
4. Harbor & Pine may send a replacement panel subject to review. A human support agent makes the decision.
5. Do not attempt to glue, tape, or reinforce a cracked panel for continued use.

## Missing Screw/Hardware Workflow

1. Check all packaging, foam, and plastic bags for loose hardware before concluding a piece is missing.
2. Confirm which screws or fasteners are missing using the parts list.
3. Provide the Amazon order ID, variant, finish, and a photo showing the missing hardware location.
4. Harbor & Pine may send a hardware kit subject to review. A human support agent makes the decision.
5. Do not substitute hardware from other furniture; use only Harbor & Pine-supplied replacements.

## Wrong Finish/Color Workflow

1. Compare the received unit to the Amazon order confirmation and packing slip to confirm the ordered variant and finish.
2. Photograph the assembled or assembled-in-part unit showing the finish, including a close-up of the surface color.
3. Provide the Amazon order ID, ordered variant and finish, and the received variant and finish.
4. Harbor & Pine may arrange a return and replacement subject to review. A human support agent makes the decision.
5. Do not modify, paint, or refinish the unit while a wrong-finish claim is under review.

## Damage-Photo Requirements

When reporting damage, missing parts, or wrong finish, provide:

1. A photo of the full unit showing overall condition.
2. A close-up of the damaged area (cracked panel, broken cam lock, stripped hole, etc.).
3. A photo showing the panel number, if visible.
4. A photo of the finish surface (for wrong-finish claims).
5. A photo of the Amazon shipping label or packing slip, if available.
6. The Amazon order ID, variant, finish, and panel number (if applicable).

Photos must be well-lit, in focus, and show the issue clearly. Harbor & Pine does not promise a replacement or refund based on photos alone; all claims are reviewed by a human support agent.

## Safety Guidance: Do Not Use Structurally Damaged Shelving

- Never use shelving with cracked, split, bowed, or broken panels.
- Never use a unit with missing or damaged hardware that affects stability.
- A shelving structural failure or risk of injury requires immediate escalation to a human support agent.

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may explain the cracked panel, missing hardware, and wrong-finish workflows and list required photos. A human support agent is required to review damage photos, decide on replacement panels or hardware, approve any refund or replacement, and handle any structural-failure or injury concern.

## Mandatory Human Escalation

Escalate to a human support agent immediately for:
- Shelving structural failure or risk of injury.
- Any cracked or split panel reported during or after assembly.
- Any refund or replacement request above $150.
- Chargebacks, payment disputes, or Amazon A-to-z Guarantee claims.
- Legal threats, safety incidents, injuries, or serious complaints.
- Suspected fraud or repeated abuse.
- Any policy exception.
