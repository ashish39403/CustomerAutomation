# Shelving Product Guide

Harbor & Pine sells flat-pack shelving through Amazon. This guide lists the available shelving variants and finishes. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Shelving Variants

| Variant | Description | Typical Use |
|---|---|---|
| 3-shelf | Three fixed shelves | Books, small decor, pantry items |
| 5-shelf | Five fixed shelves | Books, media, storage bins |
| Cube 2x2 | Four cube compartments in a 2x2 grid | Toys, cubes, baskets, display |
| Cube 3x3 | Nine cube compartments in a 3x3 grid | Larger cube storage, organizers |

## Finishes

Each variant is available in four finishes:

| Finish | Description |
|---|---|
| White | Bright white finish |
| Espresso | Dark brown finish |
| Natural Oak | Light wood-grain finish |
| Gray | Medium gray finish |

## Ordering and Identification

- Customers select a variant and finish when ordering on Amazon.
- The Amazon product listing displays the variant, finish, and dimensions.
- The order confirmation and packing slip show the ordered variant and finish.
- Harbor & Pine uses no public SKUs; customers should reference the Amazon order ID, variant, and finish when contacting support.

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may share the variant and finish table and help a customer confirm what they ordered. A human support agent is required for wrong-finish or wrong-variant claims, any damaged or cracked panel, missing hardware, and any request for a refund or replacement.
