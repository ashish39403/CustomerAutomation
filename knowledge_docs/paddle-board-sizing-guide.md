# Paddle Board Sizing Guide

Harbor & Pine sells inflatable paddle boards through Amazon. This guide helps customers choose the right board size based on rider weight. Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront; all orders are placed through Amazon.

## Rider-Weight Size Guidance

Choose a board size based on the primary rider's weight. The table below is a general starting point; actual performance also depends on payload (rider plus gear), water conditions, and rider experience.

| Board Size | Recommended Rider Weight | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| 10'6" | Up to 175 lb (79 kg) | Lighter riders, teens, casual paddling, yoga, calm lakes |
| 11' | 175 lb to 225 lb (79–102 kg) | All-around use, average adult riders, mixed conditions |
| 12'6" | 225 lb and up (102 kg+) | Heavier riders, touring, carrying gear, longer distances |

## How to Choose a Size

1. Identify the weight of the person who will ride most often.
2. Include the weight of any gear, cooler, dog, or passenger that will regularly be on the board.
3. Match the total payload to the recommended rider-weight range above.
4. When a rider falls between two sizes, choose the larger board for more stability and weight capacity, or the smaller board for easier handling and transport.

## Important Notes

- These are general recommendations, not guarantees. Stability and performance vary by rider skill and water conditions.
- Do not exceed the maximum weight capacity printed on the product listing or board.
- Harbor & Pine does not provide size guarantees. If a customer receives the wrong size, they should follow the Amazon return process (see the Returns, Refunds, and Replacements guide).

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may share the size chart above and explain how to match rider weight to a board size. A human support agent is required for any size-exchange request, a dispute about whether a board fits a rider, or any policy exception.
