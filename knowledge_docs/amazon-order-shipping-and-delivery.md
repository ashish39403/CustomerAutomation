# Amazon Order, Shipping, and Delivery

Harbor & Pine sells through Amazon. All Harbor & Pine orders are placed through Amazon; Harbor & Pine does not operate a direct storefront. This guide covers order lookup, delivery tracking boundaries, and the division of responsibility between Amazon fulfillment and Harbor & Pine support.

## Order Lookup Requirements

To look up an order, Harbor & Pine support needs:

- Amazon order ID (format: typically a 3-7 digit number, another 7 digits, and a final 7 digits, separated by hyphens)
- Customer name and email associated with the Amazon account
- Product variant, finish, or board size (for shelving or paddle boards)

Automation may ask for the Amazon order ID to locate an order. Support cannot look up orders by tracking number alone, by product SKU (Harbor & Pine does not publish public SKUs), or by a shipping address without an order ID.

## Delivery Tracking Boundaries

- Delivery tracking is managed by Amazon and the carrier. Harbor & Pine support can share tracking status only as reflected in Amazon's order data.
- Automation may communicate the carrier, estimated delivery window, and delivery status as shown on the Amazon order.
- Harbor & Pine support cannot change a delivery address after an order ships, reroute a package, or guarantee a delivery date.
- If a package shows delivered but was not received, the customer should first check with Amazon and the carrier, then contact Harbor & Pine support through Amazon with the Amazon order ID.

## Amazon Fulfillment vs. Harbor & Pine Support Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Amazon | Harbor & Pine Support |
|---|---|---|
| Order placement and payment | Yes | No |
| Shipping and carrier delivery | Yes | No |
| Delivery tracking (after shipment) | Yes | Limited to Amazon order data |
| Returns and refunds processing | Yes (Amazon platform) | Case-by-case review; no automatic refunds |
| Product questions (size, variant, finish) | No | Yes |
| Missing parts and damage (product-specific) | No | Yes, with evidence |
| Assembly instructions | No | Yes |
| Warranty-style claims | No | Yes, case by case |

Harbor & Pine support is responsible for product-specific questions and issues (paddle board sizing, inflation, leaks, shelving assembly, cracked panels, missing hardware, wrong finish). Amazon is responsible for the order transaction, payment, shipping, carrier delivery, and the Amazon return window. Harbor & Pine does not promise automatic Amazon actions such as refunds or replacements.

## Return/Refund/Replacement Boundaries

- Harbor & Pine does not promise automatic refunds, replacements, or discounts.
- Any remedy is reviewed case by case and may require evidence.
- Refund or replacement requests above $150 require mandatory human escalation.
- Customers should use the Amazon return process within Amazon's return window; Harbor & Pine support reviews claims outside that window on a case-by-case basis.

## Evidence Required

When contacting Harbor & Pine support about an order, the customer should have ready:

- Amazon order ID
- Product variant and finish (shelving) or board size (paddle boards)
- Panel number (for shelving panel issues)
- Clear photos of damage, missing parts, or wrong finish
- Packing slip or shipping label photo, if available

## What Automation May Communicate vs. What Requires a Human

Automation may share order lookup requirements, delivery tracking boundaries, and the Amazon-versus-Harbor & Pine responsibility split. A human support agent is required for any refund or replacement request above $150, chargebacks, payment disputes, Amazon A-to-z Guarantee claims, delivery disputes, and any policy exception.
